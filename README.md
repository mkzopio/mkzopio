## Enterprise financial infrastructure

**Payments · Revenue · Commerce**

My focus at Zopio is to give businesses control over how money moves, revenue is collected, and commerce performs. These operations belong in a shared financial operating model, even when they span different channels, providers, and business systems.

That requires infrastructure built around the business: decisions that can be explained, execution that can be traced, and outcomes that can be verified. Companies should retain the freedom to choose their banks and payment providers without rebuilding their business logic around each integration.

The ambition is to make financial complexity manageable as an enterprise grows. Connect what already works. Establish control where it is missing. Expand where there is a clear business case.
